01 Method

Four steps. The same order, every time.

دي طريقة شغلي مع أي براند. المحتوى بييجي في الآخر.

  1. 01

    Brand Analysis

    I read the brand first: what it sells, who it talks to, and how it looks and sounds today.

    بفهم البراند زي ما هو دلوقتي: بيبيع إيه، بيكلم مين، وشكله وصوته عاملين إزاي.

    Output Brand snapshot
  2. 02

    SWOT Analysis

    Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats — all on one page, so the next decision is easy.

    بحط نقط القوة والضعف والفرص والمخاطر في صفحة واحدة، عشان القرار اللي بعده يبقى سهل.

    Output SWOT sheet
  3. 03

    Market Study

    I study the market and the competitors to find how this product or service can really sell.

    بدرس السوق والمنافسين عشان أعرف المنتج أو الخدمة هتتباع إزاي.

    Output Selling angle
  4. 04

    Content

    Only then I build the content: pillars, a calendar, hooks, posts and reels.

    بعد كده بس ببني المحتوى: الأعمدة، الخطة، الهوكات، والبوستات والريلز.

    Output Content plan

One café. One gym. The same four steps.

In one picture

The core problem

المشكلة الأساسية في صورة واحدة.

Before step 01

The start

البداية: صاحب المكان عايز إيه؟

Café

The owner asked for a digital menu that customers open from a QR code.

Gym

The owner asked for one thing: 100K views on every video.

Step 01

Brand analysis

بقرا كل اللي البراند نزّله الأول.

Café

I read all 55 Instagram posts, one by one. 45 had no caption. The link in the bio was dead. Three visual styles were mixed.

Gym

I studied the last 48 posts with their real numbers. About 75% were AI posters. Captions praised the gym and asked nothing.

Step 02 · SWOT

Strengths

إيه اللي شغال فعلًا؟

Café

A real specialty product. The first reels got 193–242 plays from only 144 followers, so the audience responds.

Gym

Reach is healthy: reels get 1,000–7,600 views with 2,366 followers. People know the coaches by name.

Step 02 · SWOT

Weaknesses

إيه اللي بيضيّع المجهود؟

Café

No captions, no menu link, posts at 2 AM, and an 8-month gap with nothing posted.

Gym

Views do not turn into followers or messages. 34 of 37 designs got zero comments.

Step 02 · SWOT

Opportunities

فين الفرصة اللي محدش ماسكها؟

Café

The World Cup — the café shows matches on a big screen — and Saudi National Day.

Gym

Reels with real people get about 4.6× the views of AI posters. Posts that ask a question get the most comments.

Step 02 · SWOT

Threats

مين وإيه اللي ممكن يأذينا؟

Café

A competitor in the same city has 18,000 followers. A national chain has 139,000.

Gym

Five local gyms: one sells a month for 100 EGP, one is ladies-only, one is a luxury club.

Step 03

Market study

بقارن بالمنافسين وأشوف إيه اللي شغال.

Café

I compared three competitors: how often they post, how they write, and what gets engagement.

Gym

I studied 5 local and 13 global gym accounts and found 8 content patterns that work.

Step 03

The selling angle

القرار: هنبيع إزاي؟

Café

Fix the path before the posts: profile → menu → order. Content comes after the customer has somewhere to go.

Gym

The problem is conversion, not reach. Sell results, education and community — not discounts. Two branches with different hours means one is always open.

Step 04

Content

المحتوى بيتبني على كل اللي فات.

Café

A caption on every post, one visual identity, fixed posting times, and reels of the real product.

Gym

Coaches on camera. Five carousels, each with one goal: comments, shares, saves, follows, WhatsApp messages.

After step 04

What I built

التطبيق: اللي اتنفذ على الأرض.

Café

A website, plus a full system: digital menu with ordering, and the cashier.

Gym

One website for both branches, with a “Who is open now?” clock.
